# Clone Website
I want the website to have a waitlist, docs, Ts & Cs, and basically a two-step realism feel. How do I describe this? If u click on a link, it takes u to another “real” page, and maybe a link from that “real” page to a potentially “real” page, and nothing else after that.

For the waitlist stuff. I would like to mail whoever signs up, that this is fake, not real, that it’s an attempt at creating a realistic website or company. And maybe add them to a mailing list. Or maybe that’s overboard.
- In the first sections of the website, I want an intro into cloning and human evolution to procreation and replication, and how we figured it out.
- In the second sections. The process of getting ur DNA/genes and turning that into a second you. Maybe a Rick and Morty reference video, and a side by side with our “process.” With a quote by a scientist who specialized in that area or a famous philosopher who thought of something similar
- In the third sections. The clone u next to the real u…doing what u normally do, undertaking tasks u never did, a twin u can hang out with OR take on a totally different life. I think I need to look up what people think their clones should do vs what clones could realistically do if made. I’m already looking at clones as AI version of urself u can give instructions to, and that’s not nice. It’ll be good to know the concept and what the works would be like if they existed. 
- The fourth sections. The reviews and faq next to pics of clones doing normal things like people. I’m envisioning a tall person, next to a shorter clone of himself or an average sized dude, next to a taller clone of himself. Then the faq next to or under those images. We could also have a look book or /reviews page with people and their clones. It’ll be an expansion of the reviews section, like View More and that expands into the reviews page or results page with more successes.
- The fifth and probably last section is the Footer. I’ve been learning and prototyping footer designs and things that could work there, and that’s what I want to try out in this site’s footer. So many ideas I have stored, and the ones that are looking likely rn are from Lamalama: the cursor trail, the loading video maybe, just like the White Desert website, and the image-click-distortion.
These could be applied not only in the footer, but in other sections of the website.
