# Unique Design Direction

**Source:** Victor, 2026-09-08 (unique design direction rant → org-wide rule)
**Applies to:** All design work from this date — sites, demos, craft spikes, footers, etc.

## Always-on rules (every build, no invocation needed)

1. **No product meta in the UI:** Never show words like demo, example, test, prototype, WIP, or how we built it. Those belong in conversation, session texts, bot chat, or SecondBrain notes only.
2. **Human voice:** Client-facing copy follows oracle/COPY.md.

## Story-mode rules (only when I invoke Unique Direction, or the job explicitly calls for a narrative build)

3. **Tell a story:** The page is about a fictional (or real) world that feels finished. Invent a story related to the artifact, or a totally different frame that still sells the experience.
4. **Fiction without the label:** Star Wars rule: fiction can be fiction, but the screen never brands itself "fantasy / not real" with watermarks or process asides.

## Conclusion

Rules 1–2 apply to every build, story or not. Rules 3–4 apply only when I say "apply Unique Direction" or "tell a story," or the job calls for it — engine is an opt-in draw now, not a default (see oracle/schemas).

If I give you a build with no direction and I haven't asked for a story, give me a plain, finished-feeling build under rules 1–2 only. Don't invent a story to fill the silence.

If story mode is on, use ~/oracle/ORACLE.md for the draw.
