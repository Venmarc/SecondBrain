# Unique Design Direction

**Source:** Victor, 2026-09-08 (unique design direction rant → org-wide rule)  
**Applies to:** All design work from this date — sites, demos, craft spikes, footers, etc. Any work that I delegate to agents without specifying much of a direction.

## Rules

1. **No product meta in the UI** — Never show words like demo, example, test, prototype, “no Rive yet,” WIP, or how we built it. Those belong in coversation, session texts, bot chat or SecondBrain notes only.
2. **Tell a story** — The page is about a fictional (or real) world that feels finished. Invent a story related to the artifact, or a totally different frame that still sells the experience.
3. **Fiction without the label** — Star Wars rule: fiction can be fiction, but the screen never brands itself “fantasy / not real” with watermarks or process asides.
4. **Human voice** — Copy goes through Copy Humanizer (or equivalent) so it doesn’t read as AI slop / lookbook paste.

## Conclusion
When I ask you, my agent in any form (from any CLI, IDE, app,) without providing a design direction or a story to tell; Invent one. Make use of ~/oracle/ORACLE.md
