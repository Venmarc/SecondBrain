## What it looks like
Painted and rendered scene work: green valleys under puffy skies, tiny floating dioramas, aerial ocean, glowing canyons, desert arches with doorways. These are illustrations, not backgrounds, and several have a clear subject and focal point.

## Replicable in code?
**NOT CODE-REPLICABLE. Generate or model instead.**

Not attempted in the demo, deliberately. The demo tested procedural techniques only. Claiming these as code-replicable would have been a false claim, and the comparison pairs that did run on rendered references all came back as different family.

## Recipe
No shader recipe. These carry composition and subject, which is exactly what a procedural gradient cannot produce. Treat the cached previews as art direction references and generate a replacement through the image tool, or build the scene if it needs to animate.
