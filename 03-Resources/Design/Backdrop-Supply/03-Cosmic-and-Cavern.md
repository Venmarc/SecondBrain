## What it looks like
Dark grounds with luminous structure: aurora curtains, vortex portals, nebula filaments, glowing rock and light shafts. Almost always a near-black or deep-tinted base so the light does the work.

## Replicable in code?
**CODE-REPLICABLE (verified)**

Demo tiles `aurora` and `nebula` verified as polished designed backdrops by vision review, with the nebula panel specifically fixed from flat-or-blank to polished by raising brightness and dropping gas contrast.

## Recipe
Ridged or absolute-value fbm for the filaments so bright lines form along noise zero crossings, plus a strong vignette to keep the edges dark. The demo tiles `aurora` and `nebula` are this, and both came back from the vision pass as polished backdrops. Watch overall brightness: the first nebula attempt read as an empty black frame with dim tendrils and needed brightness up around 1.75 with gas contrast pulled down near 1.05.
