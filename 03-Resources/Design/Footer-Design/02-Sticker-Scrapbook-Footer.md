> **One-line Summary**: Playful footer as a "sticker sheet" or scrapbook — a tilted postage stamp, a torn-paper edge, overlapping flat-vector landmark stickers. Modern, friendly, brand-led.

**Sources:** [Harshit — "Proof that footers don't have to be boring"](https://x.com/oiharshit/status/2095863570840125489) · [Harshit — "Can we make the stickers interactive?"](https://x.com/oiharshit/status/2096219209550455207) (wiredge.studio)

## Two examples
1. **Postrail** — a tilted postage stamp (perforated border; vintage photo inside: red pillar box + pocket watch) bridges the white space above a saturated coral-red footer block. Scrapbook/sticker energy; deliberately breaks the rigid grid.
2. **Wandor** — a torn-paper wavy edge reveals a flat-vector collage of world landmarks (Eiffel Tower, Sagrada Familia, Pyramids, Big Ben, Colosseum, London Eye) layered like stickers on a blue sky. The footer *is* the illustration.

## How it avoids "boring footer"
- A massive visual anchor instead of just a link list.
- The illustration tells the brand story (travel = world landmarks; mail/time = postage stamp).
- Tactile hand-made cues: torn-paper edge, tilted sticker, overlap, perforated stamp border.

## Interactive stickers — caution
The "make the stickers interactive" follow-up is a real pattern but a shipping trap. Interactive footer stickers usually means parallax/hover/tilt. Do it only if it earns its keep: keep it subtle, respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, and never let a footer animation hurt scroll performance or LCP.
