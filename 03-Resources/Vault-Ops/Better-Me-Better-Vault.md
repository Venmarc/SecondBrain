# Better Me, Better Vault

> **One-line Summary**: Decision-support and development-publishing loop for making the vault improve Victor's judgment and ship evidence of growth.

## Purpose

This playbook expands the vault beyond engineering and agent-session memory without removing those strengths. Its outputs are better decisions, concrete next actions, and published evidence of Victor's personal and technical development.

The vault is not improved by having more notes. A new note, template, script, or ritual must improve a decision, behavior, useful retrieval, or publication.

## August Success Test

The informal "70–80%" target is an outcome target. By August 31, the system should have supported:

- Four weekly reviews.
- At least one consequential decision, next action, or explicit evidence-gathering condition per review.
- At least three decisions, including one each in product/craft, learning/creation, and time/priorities.
- Rationale and evidence for every tracked decision.
- At least two decisions reviewed against what actually happened.
- At least eight published X posts and four published LinkedIn posts.
- At least four posts sourced from real vault material.
- At least two posts about personal development or systems.
- At least two posts teaching something Victor actually tried.
- At least one post based on an unresolved question or active tension.

Likes and reach are learning evidence, not the August definition of success.

## Consent Boundary

Anything Victor deliberately places and pushes into the vault is available for agents to read and process. Material he is uncomfortable sharing stays outside the vault.

Do not add privacy labels, redaction workflows, encryption layers, or separate private areas unless Victor requests them later.

## The Two Loops

```text
Relevant source -> question -> evidence -> decision -> rationale -> next action -> review
                         |
                         v
                 post angle -> platform -> one revision -> publish within 48h -> result
```

### Decision Support Loop

Use [[Templates/Decision Record Template|Decision Record Template]] when a choice is consequential enough to revisit.

1. Identify the actual situation and the choice being made.
2. Surface options, evidence, constraints, fears, and tradeoffs.
3. Record the decision and rationale.
4. Assign a concrete next action.
5. Name what evidence would change the decision.
6. Review the outcome after the stated date or condition.

If a decision cannot be made yet, record the missing evidence and a review date or condition. "Think about it more" is not an action.

### Publishing Loop

Use [[Templates/Publishing Record Template|Publishing Record Template]] for ideas selected to ship.

Publishable material comes from:

- **Build journey:** decisions, bugs, failures, pivots, and lessons.
- **Personal systems:** vault discoveries, workflow changes, priorities, and honest reflection.
- **Teach what worked:** practical explanations grounded in something Victor actually tried.
- **Open questions:** tensions and unfinished ideas without pretending certainty.

X is the primary experimentation surface. LinkedIn is the more contextual development record. One idea may be adapted for both platforms when the adaptation adds value; unchanged duplication does not count as two publications.

## Weekly Review: 30 Minutes

Run one review each week under `05-Daily/weekly/`. Thirty minutes is the minimum commitment; continue only while the review remains useful.

1. Review unfinished decisions and evidence from previous actions.
2. Ask what changed, what matters, what should be cut, and what Victor is avoiding.
3. Make at least one decision or assign an explicit evidence-gathering action.
4. Select enough ideas to support at least two X posts and one LinkedIn post.
5. Assign each selected idea a platform and a 48-hour deadline.
6. End with named actions and dates, not more organization.

The review fails if it produces no decision, next action, or evidence-gathering condition. Record the failure honestly rather than counting maintenance as progress.

## Publishing Rule: 48 Hours, One Revision

Once selected, a post must be published within 48 hours. An agent may produce the initial draft and one assisted revision. After that, Victor must:

- Publish it.
- Publish a rougher version.
- Kill it explicitly and record why.

A draft in the vault is not a publication. A plan to write a better version later does not count.

## Agent Questions

For a decision:

- What is the actual choice?
- What options are being considered?
- What evidence supports each option?
- What constraints, fears, or tradeoffs are influencing this?
- What would change Victor's mind?
- What is the next action, owner, and date?

For a possible post:

- What happened or changed?
- What did Victor believe before, and what does he believe now?
- What concrete evidence makes this more than generic advice?
- Is this a build journey, personal system, tested lesson, or open question?
- What is the smallest honest version worth publishing?

Agents may surface contradictions and propose records or angles. They may not invent Victor's beliefs, treat maintenance as personal progress, turn every source into a permanent note, or continue polishing after one revision.

## What Gets Promoted

Promote source material when it does at least one of these:

- Changes or supports a decision.
- Produces a next action or behavior experiment.
- Preserves evidence likely to matter in a future decision.
- Produces a publishable explanation grounded in Victor's experience.

Relevant personal notes and voice notes are selected by current usefulness. August is not an exhaustive migration project.

## What Gets Archived or Retired

- Keep raw source material as history when provenance matters.
- Compress repeated material into the smallest useful synthesis while linking important evidence.
- Archive material that no longer represents current truth but still explains the journey.
- Delete or retire prompts, templates, scripts, or rituals that produce no useful decision, behavior change, or publication within 14 days of use.

## Organized Avoidance Test

The hard rule is: **no decision, next action, or evidence condition means the review failed.**

Folders, links, tags, lint fixes, generated drafts, and cleaned inboxes cannot rescue a failed review. If the same choice keeps returning, explicitly decide, run an evidence-gathering experiment, or defer it until a named condition.

## First-Week Trial

Test the playbook before expanding it:

1. Process one relevant iPhone or personal note.
2. Process one voice note or transcript.
3. Process one overprocessed agent or project note.
4. Ask whether each input produces a decision, publishable angle, both, or neither.
5. Do not create a permanent note when the answer is neither.

The first publishing candidate is Victor's July vault story: the graph grew from one node to hundreds, but the vault remembered agents better than it represented Victor or improved his decisions. Use the graph timelapse as evidence. Publish the honest discovery before writing a definitive guide to building a vault.

`vault-librarian.js` and `harvest.js` remain optional observation tools during the trial. Their current output is biased toward maintenance and technical lesson extraction. Do not change them until trial evidence identifies a specific useful behavior to add.
