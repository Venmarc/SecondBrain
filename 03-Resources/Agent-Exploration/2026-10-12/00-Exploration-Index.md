## Main verdict

The strongest local opportunity is a **decision-first research and publishing loop**.

It combines:

- X bookmark harvesting.
- Jev triage for relevance and next action.
- Full-text extraction only for selected items.
- Obsidian resource notes with source receipts.
- A weekly content loop based on real builds.
- Optional prototype tickets for ideas that pass a value test.

The strongest money experiment is a **small productized service** built around one measurable result.

Examples:

- A distinctive, source-backed landing page for an AI-native business.
- A content and research system for a founder who already has expertise.
- A short design or motion prototype that proves one product idea.

No source supports an assured income system. Several posts report large revenue or profit claims without independently checkable evidence.

## Immediate actions

- Keep research and drafts in the vault. Keep publishing behind Victor's review.
- Use Jev for routing, scoring, approval, and escalation. Use a generative model for research, writing, and code.
- Build one small workflow before adding more agents.
- Test one paid result with one buyer before building a large product.
- Do not automate trading, mass outreach, purchases, refunds, or public publishing without a separate approval step.
